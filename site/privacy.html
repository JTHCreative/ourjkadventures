privacy

your details.

what we keep, why, and how to have it removed. last updated october 2026.

who we are

j+k adventures is a small enamel pin shop. you can reach us at support@ourjkadventures.com.

if you make an account

we keep your email, your name, the date you joined, and your phone number if you choose to add one. we only use your phone number if we need to reach you about an order. if you sign in with google, google tells us your email and name. you can change any of these on your details page.

when you check out while signed in, we keep a note of the order number so it can show up in your order history. later, your account will also hold your collector's path: the pins you have registered and the rewards you have earned.

accounts are run on google firebase. your password goes to firebase, and we never see it.

if you buy something

checkout happens on square's payment page. square handles your card, your shipping address and your receipt, and we never see your card number. we see your order, your name, your email and where to send it, so we can pack and post it. if you are signed in, we link the order to your account.

your bag

what is in your bag is saved in your own browser, not with us, until you check out.

what we do not do

  • we do not sell or share your details with anyone for their own use.
  • we do not run advertising trackers.
  • we only email you about your account or your orders.

the services we use

cloudflare hosts this site. google firebase runs accounts. square takes payments. our fonts come from google fonts, and the 3d pin viewer comes from jsdelivr. each sees the basic details any website sees when your browser asks it for something, such as your ip address.

removing your details

you can delete your account at any time from your account page. that removes your login and everything we keep with it. square keeps its own record of past orders, as payment rules require. for anything else, write to us at support@ourjkadventures.com and we will help.
