privacy

your details.

what we keep, why, and how to have it removed. last updated november 2026.

who we are

j+k adventures is a small enamel pin shop. you can reach us at support@ourjkadventures.com.

if you make an account

we keep your email, your name, the date you joined, and your phone number if you choose to add one. we only use your phone number if we need to reach you about an order. if you sign in with google, google tells us your email and name. you can change any of these on your details page.

when you check out while signed in, we keep a note of the order number so it can show up in your order history. when you register a pin with the code on its card, we keep which pin it is and when you added it, so it shows in your collection and counts on your collector's path, and where you put it on your pin board. deleting your account removes them.

accounts are run on google firebase. your password goes to firebase, and we never see it.

if you leave a review

when you rate a pin, we keep your stars, any words and photos you add (up to four. before a photo leaves your device, we shrink it and leave out the hidden details cameras save, like where it was taken), the pin, the date, and which account wrote it. without an account, we keep the name and email you give us instead. we only use that email to reach you about your review, and to put your review in your account if you make one with the same email later. we read every review before it appears. once it does, anyone can see the stars, the words, the photos, the month, and your first name with your last initial. never your email. you can change or remove your review and its photos on the pin's page, and deleting your account removes your reviews and photos too.

if you tap "i'm interested"

on a pin or board that is not in the shop yet, "i'm interested" adds one to its count, and the counts are shown on the site. it does not ask for your name or email. so that each person counts once, your browser gets a small cookie holding a random number, and we keep a scrambled copy of that number with the pin you picked. we also keep a scrambled copy of your ip address mixed with the day, to count how many taps come from one internet connection each day. scrambled means it can't be turned back into the number or the address. we never keep either one as it is, and none of it is used for anything else.

if you ask us to email you when something arrives

on a pin or board that is not in the shop yet, you can leave your email. we keep the address and which pin it's for, and nothing is sent then. to catch typos, we check that the part after the @ is a real place that takes email, by asking cloudflare's public address book. when the pin arrives we send one email to say so, then remove your address from that pin's list. that email has a link to stop them all. we send it through resend, a company that delivers email for websites. it sees your address and the email, and nothing else.

if you ask us to make a pin

on the pin request page, we keep the pin set you pick, what you tell us, the date, and your name and email if you choose to give them. only we read them. we use your email only if we want to write back about your request, and we never add it to a list. to stop too many requests from one place, we keep a scrambled copy of your ip address mixed with the day, the same way as "i'm interested". you can ask us to remove a request at support@ourjkadventures.com.

if you buy something

before checkout, your bag asks where to send your order and for your email. we check the address is in the u.s. and pass it to square with your order. you pay on our checkout page, in a card form that belongs to square. your card details go straight from that form to square, so we never see your card number, and square sends your receipt. we see your order, your name, your email and where to send it, so we can pack and post it. if you are signed in, we link the order to your account.

when we post your order, we email you a note that it's on its way, with the usps tracking number. it goes to the email you gave at checkout, through resend (below).

if you mark your order as a gift, your note goes with your order in square, where we read it to write the card. our own list of cards to write keeps only the order number, not your note, email or address, and the order comes off the list once the card is written. an order you didn't finish paying for comes off after two days.

your bag

what is in your bag is saved in your own browser, not with us, until you check out.

ads, and the meta cookie

we're a two-person shop, and most people find us through instagram. so we run a few ads there and on facebook, and we use a tool from meta (the company behind both) called the meta pixel to see whether those ads are working, and to show our pins to people who've already stopped by.

it's on when you visit, and you can turn it off any time: tap "turn it off" on the little cookie note at the bottom of the page, or "cookie choices" at the bottom of any page. once it's off, nothing loads from meta and we tell meta nothing about your visit. your answer is saved in your own browser.

while it's on, here's what meta hears about:

  • the pages you look at, and which pins you open.
  • when you add something to your bag or start checking out, and which pins they are.
  • when you place an order: which pins, and what it came to.
  • what any website sees, like your ip address and which browser you use, plus a cookie meta puts in your browser so it can tell it's the same visitor next time.

our own server sends meta the same notes too (meta calls this the conversions api), so the count stays right when a browser blocks the first way. with an order, that includes a scrambled copy of your email, which meta can only match if you already have an account there with the same address. scrambled means it can't be turned back into your email.

meta never gets your name, your address, your card, your gift note, or anything from your account pages. meta uses what it does get for measuring and showing ads, under its own privacy policy.

to turn it off, tap "cookie choices" at the bottom of any page and pick "turn it off". we stop right then and clear meta's cookie from this site. if your browser sends the "global privacy control" signal, it's off for you without asking. you can also change how meta uses this kind of information in your ad settings on instagram or facebook. and you can always write to us at support@ourjkadventures.com.

what we do not do

  • we do not sell your details.
  • we do not share them for advertising, except the meta part above, which you can turn off.
  • we only email you about your account, your orders, or something you asked us about.

the services we use

cloudflare hosts this site. google firebase runs accounts. square takes payments. resend sends our emails about pins you asked for, and the note when your order ships. meta measures our ads, unless you turn it off on the cookie note. our fonts come from google fonts, and the 3d pin viewer comes from jsdelivr. each sees the basic details any website sees when your browser asks it for something, such as your ip address.

removing your details

you can delete your account at any time from your account page. that removes your login, your reviews, and everything else we keep with it. square keeps its own record of past orders, as payment rules require. for anything else, write to us at support@ourjkadventures.com and we will help.
