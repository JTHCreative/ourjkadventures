returns

returns and exchanges.

we pack every pin by hand, and we really want you to love yours. if something isn't right, here's how we'll fix it. last updated november 2026.

if your pin arrives damaged or has a flaw

so sorry! write to us within 30 days of your order arriving, at support@ourjkadventures.com, with your order number and a photo of the problem. we'll make it right with a replacement, a different pin, or your money back, whichever you'd like. if we need the pin back, we'll pay for the postage.

if you'd like a different pin

we only take returns for pins that arrive damaged or with a flaw, but we're happy to swap. within 30 days of your order arriving, send the pin back unworn and still on its card, and we'll mail you a different pin of the same price. just email us first so we know it's coming. sending it back is on you, and we'll pay to send the new one your way.

pin boards

our pin boards follow the same rules as the pins.

refunds

a refund goes back to the card you paid with, through square. it can take a few days to show up, depending on your bank.

where we ship

for now, we ship within the united states.

how to start

email support@ourjkadventures.com with your order number and what happened, or which pin you'd like instead. we'll write back with what to do next.
