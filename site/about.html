about us

the things you love.

everyone you pass on the street is carrying more than you can see. the band they cannot stop playing. the trail they want everyone to hike. the dog they would do anything for.

none of it shows. two people who love the same album never find out. a pin is the smallest way to put a piece of what you love on your outside, so the right people can find it.

a little flag that says, this one is mine.

we are j+k adventures, the two of us. we make small enamel pins, one specific thing at a time: a dog, a hometown, a year. we would rather make a pin for your trail than a pin for hiking.

and a pin should have somewhere to live. so we are making boards for the wall, starting with the bay area, so a growing collection looks like a collection and not a pile.

we make every piece to sit next to something you care about. if you love the thing, we hope you are glad we made it.
