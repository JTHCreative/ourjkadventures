about us

who we are.

we're justin and kitty, the j and k behind j+k adventures! this story started on the road. everywhere we traveled, we collected a pin to remember it, but we could never really find ones that were as beautiful as the memories.

so we made our own. we decided to make pins for the places we've been and the things we love, and a board to hang them on so our adventures are always in sight and we never forget the beautiful moments we had.

when our friends and family saw the board, we saw how it inspired them to share their journey too! it turns out pins are a really beautiful way to show who you are and hold onto the moments that matter. we hope they do the same for you!

thank you for being a part of our journey. we're excited to help you share yours.
