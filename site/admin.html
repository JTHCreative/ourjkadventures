shop settings.

    drag the sections into the order you like, or pick one and use the arrow keys. press the arrows icon again when you're done.

    the shop

    shop open

    while the shop is closed, every pin reads "coming soon" and nobody can check out. a change reaches every visitor within a minute.

    orders to ship

    paid website orders that haven't gone out yet, oldest first. a gift shows its card to write: write it by hand, tuck it in, and press "card written". then type the usps tracking number and press "mark shipped": the buyer gets the "on its way" email with a tracking button, the order is marked shipped in square, and a gift's card counts as written.

    order history

    website orders already marked shipped, and ones refunded before they shipped, newest first, read from square when you open this. "remove from history" only hides one here: it stays in square for your records.

    where buyers come from

    what buyers picked for "how did you hear about us?" at checkout, added up from the website orders in square. "paid" is what they paid in all (pins, shipping, tax and any tip), less refunds. an order refunded in full doesn't count. sales at the booth aren't asked, so they aren't here.

      by month

      products and square

      the switches by each name say whether it's in the shop. available off: it's off sale and shows "i'm interested". pre-order on: it's being made, and people can buy it now with a "pre-order" button, whatever available says. it isn't limited by stock, and square's "sold out" tick doesn't matter, but it needs a square link. switching pre-order on asks for the date it's expected in stock and shipping, and it only turns on once that's saved; buyers see "pre-order. expected in stock and shipping around december 15." the gear changes the date. collectors first (pins only): it's for sale, but only collectors with a first look (three pins registered) can buy it; everyone else sees it with its price and "with our collectors first." switch it off to let everyone buy. if the date passes, it goes back to "being made now. coming soon." until you set a new one. a bag with a pre-order ships all together around its date. turn pre-order off when the pins arrive. each saves as soon as you flip it.

      interest and emails

      everything people have asked for, in the same order as the shop's "most asked for" card: first the ones it counts (not available and not on pre-order, most taps first; it shows the top six), then the rest. a product switched to not available (in products and square) shows an "i'm interested" button in the shop: a tap adds one, once per browser, and tapping again takes it back. the counts show in the shop. after tapping, people can leave their email; nothing is sent then. when the product is back, switch it on, open the shop, and press send: everyone waiting gets one email, then comes off the list.

      pin requests

      pins people asked us to make, from "don't see your pup in the pack?" under the pup club board (the pin request page). newest first. name and email are optional, so not every one has them. nothing is sent to them: write back yourself if you'd like to. remove one once you've read it or answered it.

      one moment.

      code cards

      the little card that goes with every pin we sell. each card has its own code: the first three letters say which pin, the rest is random. the buyer types it in (or scans the card) at ourjkadventures.com/register and the pin joins their collection, once, for one account. make a batch here, download it, and send the file to whoever prints the cards: it has each code and the link its QR code should open. a card only ever goes out with a pin.

      one moment.

      look up a card

      type a card's code to see whether it's been registered, and by whom. a lost or misprinted card that nobody has registered can be cancelled, so it stops working.

      collectors

      who has reached what on the collector's path. three pins: a first look at new pins. switch a pin to "collectors first" in products and square and only these collectors can buy it, until you switch it off; copy their emails to tell them it's there. six pins: a little extra with their next order; it shows on that order in "orders to ship", and marking it shipped ticks it off. ten pins: a pin on us, once, which they pick themselves at checkout; it shows as its own line on the order, and you take one off that pin's count in square by hand.
