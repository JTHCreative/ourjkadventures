shop settings.

    drag the sections into the order you like, or pick one and use the arrow keys. press the arrows icon again when you're done.

    the shop

    shop open

    while the shop is closed, every pin reads "coming soon" and nobody can check out. a change reaches every visitor within a minute.

    orders to ship

    paid website orders that haven't gone out yet, oldest first. a gift shows its card to write: write it by hand, tuck it in, and press "card written". then type the usps tracking number and press "mark shipped": the buyer gets the "on its way" email with a tracking button, the order is marked shipped in square, and a gift's card counts as written.

    order history

    website orders already marked shipped, newest first, read from square when you open this. each one has its tracking number.

    products and square

    the switches by each name say whether it's in the shop. available off: it's off sale and shows "i'm interested". pre-order on: it's being made, and people can buy it now with a "pre-order" button, whatever available says. it isn't limited by stock, and square's "sold out" tick doesn't matter, but it needs a square link. switching pre-order on asks for the date it's expected in stock and shipping, and it only turns on once that's saved; buyers see "pre-order. expected in stock and shipping around december 15." the gear changes the date. if the date passes, it goes back to "being made now. coming soon." until you set a new one. a bag with a pre-order ships all together around its date. turn pre-order off when the pins arrive. each saves as soon as you flip it.

    interest and emails

    everything people have asked for, most asked for first. a product switched to not available (in products and square) shows an "i'm interested" button in the shop: a tap adds one, once per browser, and tapping again takes it back. the counts show in the shop. after tapping, people can leave their email; nothing is sent then. when the product is back, switch it on, open the shop, and press send: everyone waiting gets one email, then comes off the list.
