    the shop

    shop open

    while the shop is closed, every pin reads "coming soon" and nobody can check out. a change reaches every visitor within a minute.

    orders to ship

    paid website orders that haven't gone out yet, oldest first. pack one, type the usps tracking number and press "mark shipped": the buyer gets the "on its way" email with a tracking button, and the order is marked shipped in square.

    one moment.

    gift cards to write

    paid orders marked as a gift, newest first. write the note on a card and tuck it in, then press "card written" to take it off the list. the same note is on the order in square, under shipping.

    one moment.

    products and square

    the switches by each name say whether it's in the shop. available off: it's off sale and shows "i'm interested". pre-order on: it's being made, and people can buy it now: it says "pre-order. ships in about 4 weeks." and isn't limited by stock, whatever available says. it needs a square link, and must not be ticked sold out in square. the whole order ships together once it's made. turn pre-order off when the pins arrive. each saves as soon as you flip it.

    i'm interested

    a product switched to not available above is off sale, open or closed, and shows an "i'm interested" button instead. a tap adds one, once per browser, and tapping again takes it back. the counts show in the shop. most asked for first.

    one moment.

    email when it's here

    people can leave their email on a product that's not in the shop yet. nothing is sent then. when it's back, switch it on, open the shop, and press send: everyone waiting gets one email, then comes off the list.

    one moment.

    featured in the shop

    pick up to eight to turn in the carousel at the top of the shop, in the order you pick them. only pins and boards in stock can be picked. a pick that sells out steps out of the carousel until it's back in stock.

    one moment.

    products to feature

    product photos

    your own photos of a product, shown under the pup board with the photos from approved reviews (yours first). they show within a minute or so of uploading. each is shrunk to 1600px and the camera's hidden notes, like where it was taken, are dropped.

    one moment.
